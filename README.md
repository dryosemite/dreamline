# Dreamline — Ideal Lifestyle Design

A frictionless, local-first web application for Tim Ferriss's **Dreamlining** and **Ideal Lifestyle Costing** methodology from *The 4-Hour Workweek*.

**Live Demo:** https://dryosemite.github.io/dreamline/

---

## What It Does

Dreamline replaces messy spreadsheets and vague wishlists with a fast 5-step flow and real-time cash-flow feedback:

1. **Dream (Have · Be · Do)** — Run **6-month** and **12-month** sprints side by side with Tim's 5-unblocker prompts and an inline **Being → Concrete Doing** converter.
2. **Focus (Top 4 Guardrail)** — Star up to **4 life-changing dreams** per timeline to eliminate overwhelm.
3. **Cost (Cash Flow & Expenses)** — Price dreams as monthly cash flow (recurring, prorated one-time `÷ 6` or `÷ 12`, financed/leased loan calculator, or free), paired with **Quick Estimate** or Tim's **Full 21-Item Expense Audit** and customizable **1.3× safety cushion**.
4. **Reality (TMI & TDI)** — Reveal your live **Target Monthly Income (TMI)**, **Target Daily Income (TDI = TMI ÷ 30)**, cash-flow composition bar, 6M vs. 12M comparison, and **Relative Hourly Income (`$/hr`)** gap.
5. **Launch (Now · Tomorrow · Day After)** — Turn your Top 4 into immediate micro-actions with a built-in **5m / 10m Focus Timer** and countdown tracker.

### Extras Built In
- **`Tim's Sample` Preview Mode** — Explore an authentic *4-Hour Workweek* example safely without overwriting your own saved Dreamline.
- **Fear-Setting (Chapter 3)** — Built-in Define / Prevent / Repair & Cost of Inaction worksheet.
- **Local-First & Portable** — Stored in `localStorage` (zero login required) with **1-Page Printable PDF**, **Copy Markdown**, and **JSON Backup / Restore**.
- **Light & Dark Themes** + multi-currency formatting (`USD`, `EUR`, `GBP`, `BRL`, `ARS`, `CAD`, `AUD`, `MXN`).
